# Activity: Creating a stakeholder analysis matrix

## Step 1: Define the objectives of the workshop

### Workshop Objectives:

    	*Objective 1: Identify key business processes impacted by the SAP S/4HANA implementation.*  
    	Objective 2 **(Data Standardization):** Define and reach a consensus on unified data standards for customers and products across Sales, Product Development, and Support departments by the end of the session to eliminate fragmented data.  
    	Objective 3 **(Efficiency Alignment):** Identify and document at least five specific automation requirements that will reduce manual data entry and improve response times within the sales order cycle.  
    	Objective 4 **(Customer Experience):** Map the end-to-end customer interaction journey to identify integration points required for a consistent customer experience across all touchpoints. 

## **Step 2: Design Engaging Activities to Facilitate Requirements Gathering**

### **Activity 1: Process Mapping**

* **Description:** Process mapping is a technique used to visually represent the current state of a business process. Stakeholders from sales, customer support, and IT collaborate to draw a diagram of the current process, including all steps and decision points. This can be done using whiteboards or digital tools.  
* **Expected Outcomes:** Innit-Tech can identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies. This reveals gaps in the current system and highlights requirements for the new SAP S/4HANA system, such as preventing duplicate data entry.

  ### **Activity 2: User Stories & Pain Point Identification**

* **Description:** Participants write "User Stories" from the perspective of their roles (e.g., "As a Support Agent, I need a single view of customer history so that I can provide consistent answers"). Participants then use red stickers to mark "Pain Points" on the existing process maps created in Activity 1\.  
* **Expected Outcomes:** A prioritized list of user-centric requirements that directly address the most critical frustrations of the staff.

  ### **Activity 3: Future State Prototyping**

* **Description:** Using low-fidelity sketches or digital mockup tools, teams design the "ideal" screen layout or workflow for a specific task (e.g., launching a new product) in the future SAP S/4HANA system.  
* **Expected Outcomes:** Visualization of the desired user experience and clarification of functional requirements for the development team.
