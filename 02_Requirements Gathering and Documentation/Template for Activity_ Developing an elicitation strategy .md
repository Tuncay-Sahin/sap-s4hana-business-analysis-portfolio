## Step 2: Select appropriate elicitation techniques for the scenario

| Elicitation Technique | Rationale |
| :---- | :---- |
| *Interviews* | *Interviews are effective for gathering detailed, individual perspectives from key stakeholders in Finance and Procurement.* |
| *Workshops* | Essential for facilitating collaborative discussions between Finance and Supply Chain teams to reach consensus on integrated "To-Be" processes. |
| Surveys (Questionnaires) | Efficient for collecting input from many stakeholders quickly, such as gathering feedback on pain points from the wider warehouse and logistics staff. |

Step 3: Develop a plan for conducting the elicitation activities, including timelines and resources

Elicitation Plan Outline \- Major Dynamix SAP S/4HANA Implementation

Key Elicitation Activities: 

* *Conduct interviews with Finance and Procurement Managers.*  
* *Facilitate cross-functional workshops to map "As-Is" versus "To-Be" supply chain processes.*  
* *Perform Document Analysis on existing financial reporting templates and disparate system logs to identify data gaps.*

Timeline Considerations: 

* *Allocate 2 weeks for initial interviews and survey distribution to ensure high participation rates.*  
* *Schedule follow-up validation sessions after each workshop to confirm requirements before finalizing the BRD.*

Resource Considerations: 

* *Access to Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) from Finance, Procurement, and Production departments for technical deep-dives.*  
* Collaboration tools such as digital whiteboards (Miro) and a centralized requirements management repository (SharePoint).
