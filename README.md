# SAP S/4HANA Business Analysis & Strategic Solution Design Portfolio

Welcome to my professional portfolio. This repository showcases end-to-end (E2E) Business Analysis artifacts, Enterprise Architecture, and Strategic Solution Designs developed through rigorous enterprise scenarios (including GreenHarvest Organics and specialized Laboratory Reagent Inventory management systems).

The methodologies implemented here leverage **SAP S/4HANA**, **SAP Fiori UX**, and **SAP Solution Manager** frameworks to drive digital transformation, business process optimization, and robust compliance management.

## Portfolio Architecture & Modules

### [01. Foundations of Business Analysis](./01_Foundations%20of%20Business%20Analysis)
* **Focus:** Enterprise Problem Definition, Stakeholder Analysis, and Business Case Evaluation.
* **Core Scenario:** Identifying supply chain and inventory challenges to justify SAP S/4HANA transitions.

### [02. Requirements Gathering and Documentation](./02_Requirements%20Gathering%20and%20Documentation)
* **Focus:** Requirement Engineering, User Persona Definition, and Backlog Management.
* **Core Scenario:** Translating complex laboratory operations into Agile User Stories and Functional Requirements.

### [03. Business Process Modeling and Analysis](./03_Business%20Process%20Modeling%20and%20Analysis)
* **Focus:** Business Process Re-engineering (BPR), "As-Is" vs. "To-Be" analysis.
* **Core Scenario:** Modeling compliant, optimized inventory workflows using BPMN 2.0 standards.

### [04. Strategic Analysis and Solution Design](./04_Strategic%20Analysis%20and%20Solution%20Design)
* **Focus:** SAP System Architecture, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and Value Proposition.
* **Core Scenario:** Architecting secure system landscapes involving **SAP Security Analyst**, **SAP Compliance Officer**, and **SAP Data Auditor** personas.
